Knot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026 · Contact: 1806tues@gmail.com

Knot is a private app for couples. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Profile: your first name and phone number (used so your partner and status-check requests can find you; never shown publicly).
  • Content you create: messages, photos, daily-question answers, check-ins, date swipes and matches — shared only with your linked partner (and, if you opt in to Double Dates, your matched couple's group chat).
  • Relationship status: whether your account is linked to a partner. It is revealed to someone else only when you explicitly approve their status-check request; approvals expire after 24 hours. We never keep a public record of your relationship history.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics SDKs.
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.
  • Camera and microphone are used only during voice/video calls you start or answer, and for photos you choose to share. Calls are peer-to-peer (WebRTC) and are not recorded or stored.

Where data lives

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and encrypted in transit (TLS). Sign-in is anonymous device-based authentication; we do not collect your email or password.

Breakups & deletion

  • Either partner can sever the link instantly, without the other's approval.
  • You can delete your account and profile data any time in the app: Ring tab → Delete my account & data.
  • You can also email 1806tues@gmail.com for deletion requests; we honour them within 30 days.

Children

Knot is for users 18 and older.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll note it here with a new date.
